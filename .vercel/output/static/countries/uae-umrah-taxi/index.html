Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pay in SAR?

Yes, while our official rates are in SAR, we accept SAR equivalent based on daily exchange rates. You can also pay via card or cash.

Do drivers speak Arabic + English?

Our drivers are multi-lingual. For UAE pilgrims, we assign drivers who can communicate effectively in Arabic + English or English.

Is the ride private?

Absolutely. This is a VIP Private Chauffeur service. You get the whole vehicle for yourself and your family. No sharing.

How do I book from UAE?

You can book online using the form above or simply WhatsApp us. We will confirm your ride instantly.
